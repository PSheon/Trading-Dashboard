# Delete your Orbie account

Last updated: October 5, 2026

## Overview

This page explains how to delete your Orbie account and what happens to your data. You can delete your account at any time, directly in Orbie. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

## Before you delete

Your money is never deleted with your account. The wallet Privy creates for you at sign-in is your Hyperliquid account; your funds always stay in it, and Orbie cannot move them. Before deleting, go to Settings › Account, export your private key and keep it safe; with it you can recover your funds in any wallet.

Only things still in progress stop a deletion; your history never does:

- A testnet copy that is running or paused: stop it in your Portfolio and return its funds to your main wallet.
- A copy that is still stopping (cancelling orders, closing positions, returning funds): wait until it finishes.
- A one-click copy being set up whose deposit was already sent: wait until it finishes or fails.
- A deposit, a return to your main wallet, a main-wallet withdrawal or a referral reward claim still being confirmed: wait until it completes.
- An order still being confirmed: this usually takes a few minutes.
- A copy account that still holds funds, positions or orders: return the funds to your main wallet first.

Paper copies don't stop it; they are deleted with your account. Things that were never sent (an unsigned deposit, a one-click setup that hadn't started) are cancelled automatically. If something stops the deletion, the dialog tells you what it is and gives you a button to go and sort it out.

## How to delete your account

In Orbie: 1. Open Settings. 2. On a phone, tap the row at the top showing your email address; on desktop, choose Account. 3. Tap Delete account. 4. Read the notice, tick the confirmation, type DELETE and press Delete account permanently. Your account is deleted immediately.

## What is deleted

The following is removed as soon as you delete your account: your Orbie account (email, wallet address records, display name, language, role and Privy account ID); your saved traders, groups and alert settings; your Telegram link and any alerts waiting to be sent; your personal alert history; your paper copies and paper balance; invite codes nobody signed up with. If you sign in again the same way later, a brand-new account is created.

## What is kept

- **Financial records, anonymously, for 1 year:** testnet copies, copy accounts, orders and fills, deposits and returns, main-wallet withdrawals, invite relations and authorization records. They involve money, so they have to stay available for reconciliation and disputes. When you delete your account they are moved under an anonymous number that carries no personal data, so they no longer point to your email, name or sign-in; after 1 year they are deleted together with that number. People you invited, or who invited you, only see "a deleted user".
- **Protection against invite farming, for 1 year:** one keyed hash each of your Privy account ID, email and wallet addresses (computed with a key only our server holds). It can't be turned back into your details or used to look you up. Its only use: if the same identity signs up again within the year, the new account works normally but can't bind an invite code, so nobody can farm invite rewards by deleting and signing up again. It is deleted automatically after 1 year.
- **A deletion record, for 1 year:** account number, time of deletion and counts such as saved traders and alerts, with no email or addresses, kept for security auditing.
- **Your wallets:** your Privy sign-in, embedded wallet and copy-account wallets are not deleted, because your funds are in them. If a copy account had the automatic return switched on, Orbie's signing permission is removed from that wallet first; deleting never moves funds. If you want your Privy sign-in data deleted too, export your private key or move your funds out first, then contact us.
- Public Hyperliquid blockchain data is outside our control and cannot be deleted by us.
