## About Orbie

### What is Orbie?

Orbie is a trader-monitoring and copy-trading platform for Hyperliquid. You can study traders' performance and positions, and copy them with paper or actual funds.

### What does "public beta" mean?

Orbie is in public beta, so features and wording may still change.

### What is Hyperliquid?

Hyperliquid is an on-chain exchange for perpetual futures and spot trading. Every fill and position on it is public.

### Which markets does Orbie support?

Only Hyperliquid for now, including perpetual futures on crypto, stocks and other markets.

## Data sources

### Where does Orbie's data come from?

All of it comes from Hyperliquid's public API and official leaderboard. Orbie needs no permission from traders and gets no private data.

## Copy trading

### How does copy trading work?

Pick a trader and set an amount and a direction; when they fill an order, Orbie places yours by your settings. Paper copying uses virtual funds, and actual copying sends real orders on Hyperliquid.

## Fees

### How does Orbie make money?

Browsing, alerts and paper copying are free. The platform fee for actual copying is the one shown when you start a copy, and Hyperliquid's trading fees are separate.

## Safety of funds

### Are my funds safe? Who controls my wallet?

You control your wallet: Privy holds the private key, and Orbie can neither see it nor move your funds. You can export the key in Settings at any time.

### Can Orbie touch my funds when it trades for me?

Orbie only gets a trading agent that can place and cancel orders, never withdraw or transfer funds. The automatic return can only send a copy's funds back to your main wallet, and you can revoke it at any time.

## Risks

### What are the risks of copy trading?

Past performance doesn't predict the future, and leverage, delay and slippage can all cause losses. Only put in what you can afford to lose entirely.

### Does Orbie give investment advice?

No. Orbie's data, rankings and scores are information, not investment advice.
