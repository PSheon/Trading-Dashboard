## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account information (via Privy)

When you sign in, authentication is handled by Privy. We receive and store from Privy:

- your Privy user ID;
- your email address (if you sign in with email or Google);
- your wallet addresses, including the embedded wallet Privy creates for you and any external wallet you sign in with;
- account settings: display name, language preference and account role;
- when your account was created and when you last signed in.

We **never** receive or store your wallet's private key or seed phrase, your Google password or your email login codes.

### 2.2 Your settings in the Service

- the trader addresses you favorite;
- alert preferences (which traders, buy/sell direction, minimum size);
- copy trading settings and records (coming soon): the traders you copy, direction, amounts, limits, authorization status, and the orders and fills Orbie places for you.

### 2.3 Telegram information

When you connect Telegram, we store the chat ID of your conversation with @orbie_fun_bot so we can send alerts. Linking uses a one-time code that expires after 10 minutes. We do not read your other Telegram conversations or contacts.

### 2.4 Usage logs and technical data

- **Server logs:** our API records the request method, route, response status, processing time and a request ID for operations and troubleshooting. Logs do not include query parameters, request bodies or sign-in credentials.
- **Hosting logs:** our hosting providers may automatically record IP addresses, browser type (user agent) and access times in their systems.
- **Admin audit logs:** changes administrators make to site settings and user accounts are recorded.

### 2.5 Public blockchain data

The trader data Orbie analyzes (addresses, fills, positions, account value, PnL) comes from data Hyperliquid makes public. The activity of your own wallet address on Hyperliquid is equally public and can be read by anyone, including Orbie. Addresses that users favorite are monitored continuously, and their fills are stored long term to calculate performance.

### 2.6 Cookies and browser storage

- We use one cookie to remember your language.
- We use your browser's localStorage to remember interface preferences, such as announcements you have dismissed.
- Privy stores data in your browser to keep you signed in.
- We do not currently use third-party advertising or behavioral tracking cookies. 【待填：list any analytics tools here if added later】

## 8. Security

- Private keys exist only in Privy's secure infrastructure. Orbie's servers and website never see them.
- Once copy trading launches, the trading permission Orbie holds is restricted by Privy policies to trading actions such as placing and cancelling orders. It does not allow withdrawals or transfers to outside addresses.
- Traffic between the website and the API is encrypted. The service keys our servers use to talk to each other never reach the browser.
- Server logs mask keys and sign-in credentials. Access to logs and the admin area is limited to authorized staff.

No system can guarantee absolute security. If a data breach may affect your rights, we will notify you as required by law.
